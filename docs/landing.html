SET THE RULES

Choose the economic system your world runs on. You can change this during the simulation — but only when a crisis forces the question.

MODE 01
ECONOPHYSICS
"The Default World"

Everyone earns a percentage of what they already own. Money flows freely between agents. No safety net. No rules. Watch what happens when the only rule is compounding.

MODE 02
CAPITALISM
"Bet on Innovation"

Agents who accumulate enough capital can innovate — temporarily multiplying their earnings. The barrier to entry is the whole game. Can Dev make it before Jordan laps him?

MODE 03
COMMUNISM
"Share Everything"

All wealth is redistributed equally every round. Sounds like it solves the problem. Run it for 50 years and see whether equality is stable — or just delayed.

MODE 04
FASCISM
"Power Protects Power"

A ruling class taxes everyone else and keeps what they take. Party elites are chosen before the game begins. You might be one. You probably aren't.

MODE 05
CUSTOM
"Design Your Own"

Describe your economic policy in plain English. AI generates the rules. You run the experiment. What system would you build?
